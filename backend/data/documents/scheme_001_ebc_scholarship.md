# Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna (EBC)
**Government of Maharashtra | Higher & Technical Education Department**
**Government Resolution No.: TEM-2018/CR-142/TE-4 | Effective Academic Year 2024-2026**

<!-- PAGE: 1 -->
## Section 1: Objective and Scope of the Scheme
The Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna (commonly referred to as the EBC Scholarship Scheme) is instituted by the Higher and Technical Education Department, Government of Maharashtra. The primary objective is to provide financial assistance to economically backward class students pursuing higher and technical education across recognized institutions in the state of Maharashtra.

### 1.1 Applicable Institutions and Courses
1. Government and Government-Aided Higher and Technical Education Colleges, Polytechnics, and Universities.
2. Non-Aided / Private Unaided Professional and Non-Professional Degree and Diploma Colleges approved by AICTE, PCI, COA, BCI, or respective apex councils, and affiliated to recognized State Public Universities.
3. Courses covered include Engineering, Architecture, Pharmacy, Management (MBA/MMS), MCA, Hotel Management, Agriculture, and general degree courses (Arts, Science, Commerce).

## Section 2: Mandatory Eligibility Criteria & Quota Conditions

### 2.1 Family Income Limit
- The total annual income of the applicant's family (father, mother, and applicant) from all legitimate sources must not exceed **₹8,00,000/- (Rupees Eight Lakhs only)** for the preceding financial year.
- Income must be certified strictly through an official Income Certificate issued by an Executive Magistrate or Competent Revenue Authority (Tahsildar / Sub-Divisional Officer). Income affidavits or self-declarations are not accepted.

### 2.2 Centralized Admission Process (CAP) Requirement
- The student must be admitted strictly through the **Centralized Admission Process (CAP)** conducted by the State Common Entrance Test Cell (CET Cell) or the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE).
- **Strict Disqualification**: Candidates admitted under **Management Quota, Institute Level Quota, or against vacant seats without CAP merit** are strictly ineligible and shall NOT receive any fee reimbursement under this scheme under any circumstances.

### 2.3 Domicile and Residence
- The applicant must be a bona fide resident and possess a valid **Maharashtra State Domicile Certificate**.
- In the case of students residing along the Maharashtra-Karnataka border dispute area (865 disputed villages), an authorized certificate from the District Magistrate / Tahsildar of the border district confirming residency is required.

### 2.4 Academic Progression & Attempt Limits
- Only two children from the same family are eligible to avail benefits under this scheme.
- A student must pass the qualifying exam and cannot fail more than once in the standard course duration. For scholarship renewal, minimum 50% attendance is certified by the college principal.

## Section 3: Financial Entitlements and Reimbursement Scale

The financial reimbursement covers Tuition Fees and Examination Fees approved by the Fee Regulating Authority (FRA):

| Course Type | Annual Family Income Tier | Tuition Fee Reimbursement | Exam Fee Reimbursement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Professional Degree / Diploma (Govt / Aided) | Up to ₹8,00,000/- | 100% of Tuition Fees | 100% of Exam Fees |
| Professional Degree / Diploma (Unaided Private) | Up to ₹8,00,000/- | 50% of Tuition Fees | 50% of Exam Fees |
| Non-Professional Courses (B.A., B.Com, B.Sc) | Up to ₹8,00,000/- | 100% of Approved Tuition Fee | 100% of Exam Fees |
| Deemed Universities / Autonomous Institutes | Up to ₹8,00,000/- | 50% of FRA approved ceiling | 50% of Exam Fees |

*Note: Library fee, gymkhana fee, hostel deposit, and caution money deposits are strictly non-reimbursable and must be borne by the student.*

## Section 4: Mandatory Documents, Application Procedure & Deadlines

### 4.1 Required Documents Checklist
Applicants must upload clear, scanned copies of original documents on the MahaDBT portal:
1. **Income Certificate**: Current financial year income certificate (<= ₹8 Lakh) issued by the Tahsildar / Sub-Divisional Officer.
2. **Domicile Certificate**: Maharashtra State Domicile Certificate issued by Competent Authority.
3. **CAP Allotment Letter**: Official CAP seat allotment letter issued by State CET Cell / DTE confirming merit admission.
4. **Previous Year Marksheets**: Marksheet of SSC (10th), HSC (12th), or previous semester qualifying exam.
5. **Ration Card / Family Declaration**: Ration card demonstrating that candidate is among the first two children in the family.
6. **Aadhaar Seeded Bank Account**: Bank passbook or cancelled cheque of an active bank account linked with Aadhaar NPCI mapper.
7. **Fee Receipt**: College admission fee receipt showing initial fee deposit.

### 4.2 Application Channel & Verification
- Applications must be submitted exclusively online via the official **MahaDBT Portal** at `https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in`.
- Scrutiny is conducted in three stages: Institute Scrutiny (College Principal), Joint Director (JD) Higher/Technical Education, and final DBT disbursement directly to the college account (Tuition fee) and student account (Exam fee).
- **Annual Deadline**: Applications open annually on 1st August and the standard deadline for submission is **31st December** of the ongoing academic year, subject to official extension gazettes by DTE.
