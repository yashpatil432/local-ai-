# Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Swadhar Yojna for SC Students
**Government of Maharashtra | Social Justice and Special Assistance Department**
**Government Resolution No.: SJD-2022/CR-88/Edu-4 | Effective 2024-2025**

## Section 1: Objective & Scope of Swadhar Yojna
The Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Swadhar Yojna is instituted by the Social Justice and Special Assistance Department, Government of Maharashtra. The scheme addresses the acute shortage of seats in government-run backward class hostels. Scheduled Caste (SC) and Navbouddha students who secure merit admission into higher professional, technical, degree, and diploma courses but do not receive accommodation in government hostels are granted direct financial assistance for meal expenses, lodging, and academic materials.

## Section 2: City-wise Financial Allowance Scale

Financial assistance under Swadhar is disbursed in two equal half-yearly installments directly into the student’s bank account via DBT based on the classification of the study center city:

| City Category | Meal Expense Allowance | Lodging / Accommodation | Books & Stationery | Total Annual Financial Grant |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier A: Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur** | ₹32,000/- | ₹20,000/- | ₹8,000/- | **₹60,000/- per year** |
| **Tier B: Other Revenue Divisional HQs (Nashik, Amravati, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar)** | ₹28,000/- | ₹15,000/- | ₹8,000/- | **₹51,000/- per year** |
| **Tier C: Other District Headquarters** | ₹25,000/- | ₹12,000/- | ₹6,000/- | **₹43,000/- per year** |
| **Tier D: Taluka & Rural Centers** | ₹22,000/- | ₹10,000/- | ₹6,000/- | **₹38,000/- per year** |

## Section 3: Eligibility Conditions & Required Documents

### 3.1 Eligibility Requirements
1. **Caste**: The student must belong to Scheduled Caste (SC) or Navbouddha category of Maharashtra State.
2. **Domicile**: Bona fide resident of Maharashtra holding an official Domicile Certificate.
3. **Family Income Limit**: Total family annual income from all sources must not exceed **₹2,50,000/- (Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand only)**.
4. **Academic Merit**:
   - Minimum **60% aggregate marks** in the 10th (SSC) or 12th (HSC) qualifying examination for general SC students.
   - For differently abled (Divyang) SC students, the minimum qualifying mark requirement is relaxed to **50% marks**.
5. **Distance Restriction**: The student’s native home / parental residence must be at least **5 kilometers away** from the educational institution where the student is studying. Local day-scholars living with family within 5 km are not eligible.
6. **Hostel Non-Allotment Proof**: Certificate from the Assistant Commissioner of Social Welfare or college hostel showing that the candidate applied for a government hostel seat but was not allotted accommodation due to lack of vacancy.

### 3.2 Required Documents Checklist
- Caste Certificate and Caste Validity Certificate issued by Competent Scrutiny Committee.
- Tahsildar Income Certificate (<= ₹2.5 Lakh).
- Domicile Certificate of Maharashtra.
- Previous qualifying examination mark sheets.
- College Admission Fee receipt and bonafide certificate.
- Registered rent agreement / hostel warden certificate with contact details.
- Aadhaar card and passbook copy of active NPCI-linked bank account.
