# Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojna (Initial Preliminary Order v1.0 - SUPERSEDED)
**Government of Maharashtra | Women and Child Development Department**
**Government Resolution No.: WCD-2024/CR-71/W-2 | Date: 28th June 2024 (SUPERSEDED / OUTDATED)**

> [!WARNING]
> NOTICE: This initial government resolution has been officially superseded and replaced by Government Resolution No. WCD-2024/CR-89/W-2 dated 1st August 2024. Certain provisions below regarding age limit, deadlines, and mandatory income certificate are obsolete.

<!-- PAGE: 1 -->
## Section 1: Initial Framework and Pilot Objective
Government of Maharashtra hereby notifies the preliminary framework for the Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojna to provide targeted financial assistance of ₹1,500/- per month to underprivileged adult women residing within the territory of Maharashtra.

### 1.1 Preliminary Age Limit (Obsolete Provision)
- The applicant woman must have completed 21 years of age and must not have exceeded **60 years of age** on 1st July 2024. Women above 60 years of age shall not be registered under this preliminary phase.
- *(Note: Later amended to 65 years by subsequent GR in August 2024).*

<!-- PAGE: 2 -->
## Section 2: Strict Documentation & Initial Application Window

### 2.1 Mandatory Income Certificate Requirement (Superseded Rule)
- All applicants without exception must obtain and furnish a fresh annual Income Certificate issued by the Executive Magistrate / Tahsildar demonstrating annual household earnings under ₹2,50,000/-. Ration cards cannot be used in lieu of an income certificate under this initial draft.

### 2.2 Prescribed Initial Deadline
- The initial deadline for submission of filled application forms under this preliminary GR was fixed strictly as **15th July 2024**. Applications received after this date shall not be considered for the first financial phase.
- *(Note: This deadline was subsequent extended and open continuously across all Anganwadi blocks under the revised guidelines).*
