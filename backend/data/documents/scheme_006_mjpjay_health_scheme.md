# Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojna (MJPJAY 2.0 Universal Health Scheme)
**Government of Maharashtra | Public Health Department & State Health Assurance Society**
**Government Resolution No.: PHD-2024/CR-198/Health-6 | Effective July 2024 (Updated February 2025)**

<!-- PAGE: 1 -->
## Section 1: Universal Healthcare Framework for Maharashtra
Under Government Resolution dated July 2024, the Government of Maharashtra expanded the Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojna (MJPJAY 2.0) to achieve Universal Health Coverage (UHC) for all bona fide residents of the State of Maharashtra.

### 1.1 Universal Eligibility (Removal of Income Cap)
- **Universal Population Coverage**: Under MJPJAY 2.0, the scheme covers **all 12.5 crore citizens of Maharashtra**.
- **No Income Ceiling Restriction**: Unlike earlier versions that restricted benefits solely to BPL/Antyodaya or Orange ration card families, the expanded scheme provides coverage to **all ration card holders** (Yellow, Orange, and White Ration Card holders alike) holding valid Maharashtra domicile.

## Section 2: Financial Sum Insured and Procedure Coverage

### 2.1 Annual Cashless Health Insurance Coverage
- **Annual Sum Insured**: Coverage has been elevated to **₹5,00,000/- (Rupees Five Lakhs only) per family per year** on a floater cashless basis (upgraded from the previous ceiling of ₹1.5 Lakh).
- **Cashless Treatment**: The entire cost of consultation, diagnostic investigations, surgical procedures, ICU charges, medicines, implants, food, and post-discharge follow-up care up to 10 days is covered completely free of cost for the patient.

### 2.2 Medical & Surgical Specialties Covered
- Total of **1,356 secondary and tertiary procedures** across 34 clinical specialties are authorized under the scheme.
- High-end treatments include Cardiology & Cardiac Surgery, Oncology (Chemotherapy, Radiation, Surgical Oncology), Neurosurgery, Orthopedic Joint Replacements, Pediatric Surgery, Nephrology (Dialysis & Kidney Transplant up to ₹3.5 Lakh package).

| Treatment Category | Package Ceiling Under MJPJAY 2.0 | Cashless Coverage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| General Secondary Surgeries (Hernia, Appendectomy) | Full Standard Tariff Package | 100% Cashless |
| Complex Tertiary Surgeries (Coronary Bypass CABG) | Full Standard Tariff Package | 100% Cashless |
| Cancer Care (Medical / Surgical / Radiation) | Full Standard Tariff Package | 100% Cashless |
| Kidney Renal Transplant | Capped package up to ₹3,50,000/- | 100% Cashless |
| Polytrauma & Critical Care Management | Up to ₹5,00,000/- family limit | 100% Cashless |

## Section 3: Empaneled Hospital Network & Arogyamitra Desk

### 3.1 Network Hospitals
- The scheme is implemented through a network of over **1,000 empaneled hospitals**, comprising:
  - Government Medical Colleges and District Civil Hospitals (100% reserved procedures).
  - Empaneled private super-specialty and trust hospitals across all 36 districts of Maharashtra.

### 3.2 The Role of "Arogyamitra"
- Every empaneled hospital maintains a dedicated, round-the-clock **Arogyamitra Help Desk** at the casualty / reception area.
- The Arogyamitra assists the patient in:
  1. Patient verification and biometrics via Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA) or Aadhaar.
  2. Online pre-authorization submission to the State Health Assurance Society.
  3. Pre-auth approval within 2 to 6 hours for emergency admissions.
  4. Cashless discharge and dispensing of free follow-up medicines.

## Section 4: Mandatory Identification Documents and Grievance Toll-Free

### 4.1 Required Identification Documents
Any prospective patient must present at the hospital Arogyamitra desk:
1. **Ration Card**: Valid Yellow, Orange, or White Ration Card issued by Maharashtra Government.
2. **Photo Identity**: Aadhaar Card (primary) or Voter ID Card / Driving License.
3. For children under 5 years: Birth Certificate and mother's/father's Aadhaar card.

### 4.2 Official Toll-Free Helpline
- Beneficiaries facing refusal or illegal billing by any empaneled hospital can immediately call the 24x7 State Health Assurance Society Toll-Free Helpline: **155388** or **1800 233 2200**.
