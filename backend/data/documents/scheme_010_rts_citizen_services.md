## Section 1: Statutory Framework of the Maharashtra RTS Act
The Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015 (RTS Act) legally empowers citizens of Maharashtra to obtain notified public services from government departments and local authorities in a transparent, accountable, and time-bound manner. Over 500 public services across Revenue, Rural Development, Urban Development, Home, and Social Welfare departments are legally notified with binding statutory delivery deadlines.

### 1.1 Objective of the Service Guarantee
Under this legislation, if a designated government officer fails to deliver a notified citizen service within the stipulated time limit without lawful reason, or arbitrarily rejects the application, the citizen possesses a statutory right to file appeals and the defaulting official is subject to mandatory disciplinary monetary penalties.

## Section 3: Two-Tier Appeal Mechanism and Statutory Penalties

### 3.1 First Appeal (RTS Appeal-1)
- If an applicant does not receive the notified service within the statutory deadline, or if the application is rejected without proper legal grounds:
- The citizen may file a **First Appeal within 30 days** from the expiry of the deadline or receipt of the rejection order.
- The First Appellate Authority (typically the Sub-Divisional Officer or Deputy Collector) must adjudicate and pass a written order within **30 days**.

### 3.2 Second Appeal (RTS Appeal-2)
- If the citizen is aggrieved by the order of the First Appellate Authority, they may file a **Second Appeal within 30 days** before the Second Appellate Authority (Additional Collector / Divisional Commissioner).
- The Second Appellate Authority can direct immediate issuance of the certificate and initiate penalty proceedings.

### 3.3 Penalty Provisions on Defaulting Government Officers
- Under Section 10 of the RTS Act, the Appellate Authority is empowered to levy a **personal penalty ranging between ₹500/- and up to ₹5,000/-** directly on the Designated Officer for unjustified delay or refusal.
- The penalty amount is recovered directly from the defaulting officer's salary.
- In addition, the Appellate Authority may award a portion of the penalty as financial compensation to the aggrieved citizen.

## Section 4: Citizen Application Portal & Helpline

### 4.1 Aaple Sarkar Single-Window Digital Portal
- Citizens can apply, track real-time application status, and download digitally signed certificates 24x7 from the official portal:
- **URL**: `https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in`
- Every application is assigned an automated 15-digit Application ID with SMS tracking alerts dispatched at every milestone.

### 4.2 State RTS Chief Commissioner and Helpline
- State Commission Head: Maharashtra State Right to Service Commission, Mantralaya, Mumbai.
- Citizen Helpdesk Toll-Free Number: **1800 120 8040** (available from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM on all working days).
- Inquiries can also be lodged at any authorized Setu Suvidha Kendra or Maha e-Seva Kendra across all talukas of Maharashtra.
