# Right to Education (RTE) 25% Free Admission Scheme Maharashtra
**Government of Maharashtra | School Education and Sports Department**
**Government Resolution No.: RTE-2024/CR-62/PE-1 | Academic Year 2024-2026**

<!-- PAGE: 1 -->
## Section 1: Statutory Mandate and School Reservations
In accordance with Section 12(1)(c) of the Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009, all recognized private un-aided primary schools in Maharashtra are legally obligated to reserve **25% of entry-level seats** (Pre-Primary / Nursery / Junior KG or Class 1 depending on school entry point) for children belonging to Disadvantaged Groups and Weaker Sections residing within the designated local neighborhood limits.

### 1.1 Neighborhood Distance Radii
- **First Priority (Within 1 KM)**: Children residing within a 1-kilometer aerial radius of the school.
- **Second Priority (1 KM to 3 KM)**: If seats remain vacant after the first lottery round, children within 3 kilometers are allocated seats.

## Section 2: Beneficiary Categories, Age Limits & Income Criteria

The eligible applicant pool is divided into two statutory categories:

### 2.1 Category A: Disadvantaged Groups (Vanchit Gat)
- Includes Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Vimukta Jati / Nomadic Tribes (VJNT), Other Backward Classes (OBC), Special Backward Classes (SBC).
- Children with disabilities (Divyang, minimum 40% certified disability).
- HIV-affected / orphan children / transgender children.
- **Income Limit for Disadvantaged Groups**: **NO INCOME LIMIT APPLIES**. Families in this category are eligible regardless of their annual income.

### 2.2 Category B: Economically Weaker Section (Durbal Gat)
- Children belonging to Open / General categories whose parents/guardians have an annual family income from all sources **below ₹1,00,000/- (Rupees One Lakh only)**.
- Income certificate issued by the Executive Magistrate / Tahsildar for the latest financial year is mandatory.

### 2.3 Age Criteria for Entry Level Admission
- **Nursery / Pre-School Entry**: Child must have completed **3 years** and not exceeded 4 years 5 months as on 31st December.
- **Class 1 (Grade 1 Entry)**: Child must have completed **6 years** as on 31st December of the admission year.

## Section 3: Centralized Online Lottery Process & Mandatory Documents

### 3.1 Centralized Computerized Lottery
- Admission is conducted strictly through an automated online computerized lottery via the state portal: `https://student.maharashtra.gov.in/adm_portal`.
- Parents can choose up to 10 schools in their neighborhood order of preference.
- Schools are strictly barred from conducting interviews, screening tests, or collecting donations/capitation fees from selected children.

### 3.2 Mandatory Document Checklist for Verification
1. **Birth Certificate**: Municipal Corporation / Gram Panchayat birth certificate or Hospital discharge card.
2. **Address Proof**: Valid Ration Card / Electricity Bill / Telephone Bill / Driving License / Domicile Certificate / Registered Rent Agreement (registered before official notification date).
3. **Caste Certificate**: In the name of the child or the child's father, issued by the Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) in Maharashtra (for Disadvantaged Group applicants).
4. **Income Certificate**: Tahsildar-issued income certificate below ₹1 Lakh per annum (for Weaker Section applicants).
5. **Divyang Certificate**: Civil Surgeon disability certificate (for children with special needs).
