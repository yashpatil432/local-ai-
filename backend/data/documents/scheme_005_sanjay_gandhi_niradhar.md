# Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojna (Financial Assistance for Destitute)
**Government of Maharashtra | Social Justice and Special Assistance Department**
**Government Resolution No.: SJD-2023/CR-112/BCW-3 | Published: November 2023 (Revised 2024)**

<!-- PAGE: 1 -->
## Section 1: Preamble and Target Beneficiary Categories
The Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojna is a state-funded welfare pension program for vulnerable, destitute, and severely distressed individuals residing in Maharashtra who lack any regular source of sustenance or family support.

### 1.1 Eligible Target Groups
1. Destitute men and women aged **65 years and above** with no adult earning progeny.
2. Destitute widows and destitute divorced women with minor dependent children.
3. Transgender individuals (Tritiyapanthi) without family support.
4. Blind persons and persons with severe physical or mental disabilities (minimum **40% disability certified** by Civil Surgeon).
5. Orphan children below 18 years of age without surviving parents or legal guardians.
6. Patients suffering from catastrophic or incapacitating diseases (advanced stage Cancer, TB, severe Leprosy, Kidney Failure requiring dialysis, advanced Parkinson's disease).

## Section 2: Strict Income Ceiling & Financial Pension Amounts

### 2.1 Income Eligibility Limit
- The total annual income of the applicant’s household from all sources must not exceed **₹21,000/- (Rupees Twenty-One Thousand only)** per annum.
- Any applicant with family income exceeding ₹21,000 per year is strictly ineligible.
- Income certificate must be certified by the local Revenue Officer (Tahsildar / Naib Tahsildar).

### 2.2 Scale of Monthly Pension Grants
The pension amount is credited directly to the beneficiary's post office or nationalized bank savings account on a monthly basis:

| Beneficiary Composition | Monthly Grant Amount | Annual Financial Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Single Individual Beneficiary** | **₹1,500/- per month** | **₹18,000/- per year** |
| **Family with Two or More Beneficiaries** | **₹2,400/- per month** (combined) | **₹28,800/- per year** |

## Section 3: Sanctioning Procedure and Required Documents

### 3.1 Sanctioning Committee Authority
- Applications are submitted to the local **Tahsildar Office (Setu Kendra)**.
- Scrutiny and official sanction are decided by the **Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Sanction Committee** constituted for each Taluka, chaired by a designated local public representative with the Tahsildar acting as the Member Secretary.

### 3.2 Required Documents Checklist
1. **Age Proof**: School leaving certificate, Birth Certificate, Voter ID, or Medical Age Certificate issued by Civil Surgeon.
2. **Residence Proof**: Domicile Certificate or proof of **continuous residence in Maharashtra State for at least 15 years**.
3. **Income Certificate**: Annual income below ₹21,000/- certified by Tahsildar.
4. **Medical Certificate**: In disability or critical illness cases, official Disability Certificate / Medical Board Certificate from District Civil Surgeon.
5. **Death Certificate of Spouse**: For widow applicants.
6. **Aadhaar Card and Bank Account Passbook**: Bank account linked with NPCI.
