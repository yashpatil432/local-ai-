# Dr. Punjabrao Deshmukh Vastigruh Nirvah Bhatta Yojna (Hostel Allowance)
**Government of Maharashtra | Higher & Technical Education and Agriculture Department**
**Government Resolution No.: HED-2021/CR-45/HE-2 | Effective 2024-2025**

<!-- PAGE: 1 -->
## Section 1: Objective of the Scheme
The Dr. Punjabrao Deshmukh Vastigruh Nirvah Bhatta Yojna is instituted by the Government of Maharashtra to provide hostel maintenance allowance to children of registered marginal farmers (Alpabhudharak Shetkari) and registered agricultural laborers (Shetmajoor) who are enrolled in higher and technical professional education institutions and are unable to obtain accommodation in government hostels.

### 1.1 Eligible Courses
Professional degree and diploma courses approved by the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Directorate of Higher Education (DHE), Maharashtra Council of Agricultural Education and Research (MCAER), and Directorate of Medical Education and Research (DMER). Non-professional general courses are not covered under this specific hostel grant.

<!-- PAGE: 2 -->
## Section 2: City Tiers, Maintenance Allowances & Benefit Rates

The scheme disburses financial allowance for 10 months during an academic year based on the geographic tier of the city in which the student is studying:

| Geographic Classification | Covered Cities & Municipal Corporation Areas | Monthly Allowance | Maximum Annual Allowance (10 Months) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1 (Metropolitan & Major Cities)** | **Mumbai Metropolitan Region, Pune, Nagpur, Nashik, Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar)** | **₹3,000/- per month** | **₹30,000/- per year** |
| **Tier 2 (Other Districts & Talukas)** | **All other District Headquarters, Municipal Council areas, and Rural Talukas** | **₹2,000/- per month** | **₹20,000/- per year** |

### 2.1 Regional Applicability: Can a Student from Nashik Apply?
- **Yes, absolutely**. Students studying in institutions located in **Nashik** (including Nashik City and Nashik Municipal Corporation boundaries) are explicitly classified under **Tier 1**, qualifying for the maximum financial benefit of **₹30,000/- per academic year**.
- Similarly, a student whose parents reside in rural Nashik district or any other Maharashtra district who relocates to an approved hostel or rented accommodation is fully eligible.

## Section 3: Eligibility Thresholds and Mandatory Documents

### 3.1 Family Income Ceilings
1. **Children of Registered Marginal Farmers (Alpabhudharak)**:
   - Landholding must not exceed **2.0 Hectares (5 Acres)** of agricultural land.
   - Total family annual income limit: **₹8,00,000/- (Rupees Eight Lakhs only)**.
2. **Children of Registered Agricultural Laborers (Shetmajoor)**:
   - Must hold a certificate from the competent revenue authority/Gram Sevak.
   - Total family annual income limit: **₹8,00,000/-**.
3. **General Open/EBC Students without Government Hostel**:
   - Total family annual income limit: **₹1,00,000/-** (entitled to ₹10,000 - ₹8,000 depending on area).

### 3.2 Required Document Checklist
1. **MahaDBT Online Registration Form**.
2. **Landholding Extract**: 7/12 extract (Satbara Utara) and 8A extract in the name of father/mother showing landholding <= 2 hectares (certified by Talathi).
3. **Income Certificate**: Issued by Tahsildar for the financial year.
4. **Hostel Certificate / Rent Agreement**: Registered hostel admission certificate and fee receipt, or registered rent agreement with house owner and local police verification.
5. **CAP Allotment Letter**: Seat allocation through centralized admission process.
6. **Attendance Certificate**: Minimum 75% attendance certificate issued by the college principal.
