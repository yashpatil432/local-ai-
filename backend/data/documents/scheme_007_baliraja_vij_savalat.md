# Mukhyamantri Baliraja Vij Savalat Yojna (Agricultural Electricity Waiver)
**Government of Maharashtra | Energy Department & MSEDCL (Mahavitaran)**
**Government Resolution No.: ENG-2024/CR-312/Power-2 | Date: 25th July 2024 (Effective 2024-2029)**

<!-- PAGE: 1 -->
## Section 1: Objective & Scope of Free Power for Farmers
The Mukhyamantri Baliraja Vij Savalat Yojna was sanctioned by the Cabinet of Maharashtra to alleviate the agrarian distress faced by farmers due to unpredictable monsoons and high production costs. The scheme provides a comprehensive **100% electricity bill waiver** for agricultural pump consumers across the state of Maharashtra.

### 1.1 Covered Agricultural Pump Capacity
- **Capacity Ceiling**: All individual farmers and registered cooperative farmer lift-irrigation groups operating agricultural electricity pumps with capacity **up to 7.5 Horse Power (HP)** are fully covered.
- Agricultural pumps above 7.5 HP capacity continue to receive the standard state agricultural tariff concession but are not covered under the 100% free power waiver.

### 1.2 Tenure of the Scheme
- The scheme is enforced for a period of **5 years**, starting retroactively from **April 2024 through March 2029**.
- The State Government has made a budgetary allocation of **₹14,761 crore annually** to compensate the Maharashtra State Electricity Distribution Company Limited (MSEDCL/Mahavitaran) for the power subsidy.

## Section 2: Implementation Guidelines & Eligibility Conditions

### 2.1 Eligibility Criteria
1. The electricity connection must be officially categorized under the **Agricultural Pump Tariff category (LT-AG or HT-AG)** in MSEDCL records.
2. The consumer must hold an active 12-digit MSEDCL Consumer Number.
3. Both metered and unmetered agricultural pumps up to 7.5 HP are entitled to the benefit.
4. Pending past arrears prior to March 2024 are sheltered under the separate Krishi Sanjeevani Amnesty scheme and do not disqualify the farmer from availing current zero-electricity billing under Baliraja Yojna.

### 2.2 Billing & Citizen Verification Procedure
- **Zero Electricity Bills**: Eligible farmers receive electricity bills clearly reflecting a **"Net Payable Amount: ₹0.00"**, with the state government subsidy line item explicitly displayed.
- **Solar Feeder Integration**: Under Mukhyamantri Saur Krishi Vahini Yojna 2.0 (MSKVY 2.0), daytime agricultural power supply (from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM) is progressively guaranteed for all Baliraja beneficiaries.
- **Inquiries & Complaints**: Farmers may verify their consumer subsidy status via the Mahavitaran Mobile App or by contacting the MSEDCL farmer toll-free desk at **1912** or **1800-212-3435**.
