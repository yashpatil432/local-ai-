# Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojna (Revised Guidelines v2.1)
**Government of Maharashtra | Women and Child Development Department**
**Government Resolution No.: WCD-2024/CR-89/W-2 | Date: 1st August 2024 (Updated January 2025)**

<!-- PAGE: 1 -->
## Section 1: Introduction and Objective
The Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojna is a flagship socio-economic empowerment scheme launched by the Government of Maharashtra under the Women and Child Development Department. The scheme provides monthly financial assistance directly into the Aadhaar-linked bank accounts of eligible women in Maharashtra to enhance their economic independence, health, nutritional security, and decision-making role within the household.

### 1.1 Benefit Amount and Mode of Payment
- Every beneficiary receives **₹1,500/- (Rupees One Thousand Five Hundred only) per month**.
- Payments are processed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) through the Aadhaar Payment Bridge System (APBS) into the beneficiary’s active, DBT-enabled bank account.

## Section 2: Revised Eligibility Criteria and Relaxations

### 2.1 Age Limit (Revised Amendment)
- **Age Window**: Women aged between **21 years completed and up to 65 years** as of the 1st of July of the application year are eligible.
- *(Note on amendment: In the preliminary June 2024 notification, the maximum age was 60 years. By Government Resolution dated 1st August 2024, the ceiling was officially expanded to 65 years to cover senior women).*

### 2.2 Income Ceiling & Major Document Exemption
- **Annual Family Income**: The combined family annual income must not exceed **₹2,50,000/- (Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand only)**.
- **Ration Card Exemption Clause**: If the applicant's family holds a valid **Yellow Ration Card** or **Orange Ration Card** issued by the Food and Civil Supplies Department of Maharashtra, they are **STRICTLY EXEMPT from submitting a separate Tahsildar Income Certificate**. The ration card itself is accepted as conclusive proof of income.
- White Ration Card holders or families without ration cards must submit an annual Income Certificate from the Tahsildar showing income under ₹2.5 Lakh.

### 2.3 Residential & Marital Status
- The applicant must be a resident of Maharashtra State.
- Eligible categories include married women, unmarried adult women (above 21), widows, divorcees, abandoned women, and destitute women.
- Only one woman per eligible family can claim the benefit if both mother-in-law and daughter-in-law apply, but unmarried sisters in separate income units can apply independently.
- Disqualifications: Women whose family members pay income tax, or whose family member is a regular/permanent government employee, or whose family owns more than 5 acres of agricultural land are ineligible.

## Section 3: Required Documents and Application Process

### 3.1 List of Required Documents
Applicants must submit or upload:
1. **Aadhaar Card**: Mandatory for identity verification and biometric e-KYC.
2. **Proof of Residence / Domicile**: Any ONE of the following:
   - Maharashtra State Domicile Certificate, OR
   - School Leaving Certificate showing birth/education in Maharashtra (minimum 15 years), OR
   - Ration Card issued in Maharashtra, OR
   - Voter Identity Card issued in Maharashtra showing 15 years residence.
3. **Income Proof**:
   - Yellow or Orange Ration Card (Direct waiver for separate certificate), OR
   - Tahsildar Income Certificate (<= ₹2.5 Lakh per year).
4. **Bank Account Details**:
   - Passbook photocopy of an individual bank account (joint accounts are not permitted) linked with Aadhaar NPCI mapper.
5. **Undertaking / Hamipatra**: Standard self-declaration acknowledging that no family member is a government employee or income tax payer.

### 3.2 Application Channels and Registration Centers
- **Online Channel**: Nari Shakti Doot Mobile Application / Aaple Sarkar portal.
- **Assisted Offline Centers**: Anganwadi Centers, Gram Panchayat offices, Setu Suvidha Kendra, Aaple Sarkar Seva Kendra, Ward offices in Municipal Corporation areas.
- **Fees**: The entire application process is completely **FREE of charge**. No service center or individual is authorized to collect fees from applicants.
