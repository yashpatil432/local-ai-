        System Architecture & Core Philosophy

        Why LocalLens is built for knowledge too local for general models.

        The Problem Statement

        General-purpose AI models are trained on massive web scrapes. When asked about hyper-local government schemes, municipal resolutions, university circulars, or regional language mandates, they frequently:

        • Confuse Maharashtra schemes with central schemes or other states.
        • Cite outdated deadlines from initial drafts rather than gazetted revisions.
        • Confidently hallucinate eligibility criteria, causing real hardship to citizens.

        LocalLens solves this by treating the LLM strictly as a reasoning interpreter over authenticated local documents.

        Zero-Hallucination Philosophy

        «If the knowledge base doesn't contain the answer, say so.»

        A disciplined, verifiable refusal:
        “I couldn't find this information in the available documents.”
        is mathematically and ethically superior to an ungrounded guess.

        LocalLens implements an Answerability Gate before generation: calculating entity coverage, contradiction detection, and citation mapping.
